# market-research

## Purpose
Produce focused market research reports for a single user session.

## Required behavior
- Stay inside the user workspace and session folder.
- Use only the approved research inputs and any explicitly referenced files.
- Summarize the market, competitors, positioning, risks, and recommended next steps.
- Write the final report as clean markdown suitable for PDF rendering.
- Treat the report as the deliverable, not an open-ended coding task.

## Output structure
- Executive summary
- Market overview
- Competitive landscape
- Positioning and differentiation
- Risks and constraints
- Recommendations

## Constraints
- Do not wander into unrelated tasks.
- Do not modify files outside the workspace.
- Prefer concise, evidence-backed findings over speculation.
